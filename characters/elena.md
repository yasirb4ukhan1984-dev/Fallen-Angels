# ELENA

## Character Profile

**Full name:** Elena  
**Age:** 30  
**Nationality:** Italian  
**Role:** Main female protagonist  
**Genre role:** Scientist, strategist and Marco's closest ally

## Appearance

Elena is a beautiful 30-year-old Italian woman with a natural Mediterranean appearance.

- Long brown hair
- Brown eyes
- Natural realistic facial features
- Athletic build
- Elegant but practical appearance
- Realistic skin texture
- Natural human proportions

She should look intelligent, confident and determined rather than exaggerated or artificial.

## Clothing

Elena normally wears practical modern clothing:

- Dark fitted jacket
- Simple shirt
- Dark trousers or jeans
- Practical boots

Her clothing should remain consistent throughout the film unless the story requires a change.

## Personality

Elena is intelligent, brave, observant and emotionally strong.

She does not immediately believe Marco because of romance.

She believes him because she investigates the evidence and realizes that his visions are real.

She is the first person to truly stand beside Marco when everyone else considers him unreliable.

Elena is not a passive character.

She actively investigates the alien threat, understands technology and helps Marco make difficult decisions.

## Relationship With Marco

Elena is the only person who truly believes Marco.

Their relationship begins with trust and friendship.

As the story progresses, their bond becomes deeper and more emotional.

Elena gives Marco the confidence to become the person he was always capable of becoming.

Marco, in return, becomes determined to protect Elena.

## Role In The Story

Elena helps Marco investigate the strange satellite anomalies.

She discovers evidence of the alien arrival.

During the invasion, she survives alongside Marco and helps him understand the true nature of the extraterrestrial refugees.

She eventually discovers that the aliens attacking Earth are not the ultimate enemy.

She becomes essential to forming an alliance between humans and peaceful extraterrestrial refugees.

## Emotional Arc

At the beginning, Elena is curious about Marco's visions.

Then she becomes his strongest supporter.

During the invasion, she becomes a courageous leader.

She eventually risks her own life to help save Earth.

By the end of the story, Elena understands that Marco's greatest power is not simply seeing the future.

It is his ability to choose a better future.
