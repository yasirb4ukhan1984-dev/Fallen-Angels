# MARCO

## Character Profile

**Full name:** Marco  
**Age:** 35  
**Nationality:** Italian  
**Role:** Main protagonist  
**Genre role:** Reluctant hero

## Appearance

Marco is a handsome 35-year-old Italian man with a Mediterranean appearance.

- Dark brown, slightly messy hair
- Short, well-groomed beard
- Brown eyes
- Strong but natural facial features
- Average athletic build
- Realistic proportions
- Natural skin texture
- No exaggerated superhero physique

## Clothing

Marco normally wears:

- Dark jacket
- Simple dark T-shirt
- Dark jeans
- Simple black or dark sneakers

His clothing should remain consistent throughout the film unless the scene specifically requires a change.

## Personality

Marco is intelligent, quiet and observant.

He has never been successful in life.

People often underestimate him.

He is used to nobody believing in him.

Despite this, Marco has a strong sense of responsibility and refuses to abandon innocent people.

He does not initially want to be a hero.

He simply wants to save the people he loves and protect Earth.

## Ability

Marco can see fragments of the future.

His visions can show:

- A few seconds into the future
- Possible future events
- Different possible timelines
- Events involving people close to him

His visions are not always clear.

Changing one event can change the future he previously saw.

This creates uncertainty and suspense throughout the film.

## Emotional Arc

At the beginning of the story, Marco believes he is an ordinary man nobody needs.

During the alien invasion, he gradually discovers that his ability may be the key to saving humanity.

Elena is the first person who truly believes in him.

By the end of the story, Marco accepts his responsibility and becomes the hero who saves Earth.
