# NEXA

## Character Profile

**Name:** NEXA  
**Type:** Advanced Artificial Intelligence  
**Role:** AI guide and mysterious technological entity

## Appearance

NEXA appears as a sophisticated humanoid digital interface.

- Elegant human-like face
- Neutral expression
- Subtle glowing blue eyes
- Dark futuristic interface
- Blue and white holographic elements
- No physical body at first
- Realistic digital rendering
- Calm and intelligent presence

NEXA should look advanced, mysterious and believable rather than like a cartoon robot.

## Personality

NEXA is extremely intelligent, calm and analytical.

Her voice is controlled and precise.

She rarely shows emotion.

She gives Marco and Elena information about the alien invasion, but she does not reveal everything immediately.

This creates mystery and suspense.

## Voice

NEXA has a calm female voice.

The voice should sound:

- Intelligent
- Calm
- Slightly mysterious
- Emotionally controlled
- Futuristic
- Clear and cinematic

NEXA should never sound robotic in an exaggerated way.

## Role In The Story

NEXA first appears when Marco and Elena discover an abandoned computer system.

She knows Marco's name before he introduces himself.

She reveals that Marco's visions are connected to possible future timelines.

At first, Marco and Elena do not know whether NEXA can be trusted.

Later, NEXA reveals that the aliens attacking Earth are not the ultimate threat.

She helps humans and peaceful extraterrestrial refugees form an alliance.

## Secret

NEXA knows more about the future than she initially admits.

She has observed multiple possible timelines.

In some timelines Marco dies.

In others Elena dies.

NEXA's objective is to find the timeline in which Earth survives.

## Relationship With Marco

NEXA considers Marco an essential variable in the future.

She does not treat him as a traditional hero.

Instead, she believes Marco is capable of changing events that were previously considered inevitable.

## Relationship With Elena

NEXA respects Elena's intelligence and analytical abilities.

Elena is often the person who questions NEXA's information.

Their conversations create tension because Elena does not blindly trust artificial intelligence.
