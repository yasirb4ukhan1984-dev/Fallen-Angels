# FALLEN ANGELS
## Episode 01 — The Future

### Genre
Sci-Fi / Action / Suspense / Drama

### Setting
Rome, Italy

### Format
4K cinematic, photorealistic, 16:9

---

## SCENE 01 — ROME, 3:17 A.M.

Marco, 35, a handsome Italian man with dark brown messy hair,
short beard and an average athletic build, walks alone through
Rome at night.

The streets are wet. The Colosseum is illuminated in the distance.

### Dialogue

MARCO — VOICE OVER:

"Sometimes I think the world keeps moving...
even when something is about to end."

Marco suddenly stops.

A strange high-pitched sound surrounds him.

---

## SCENE 02 — THE FIRST VISION

Marco closes his eyes.

He suddenly sees a terrifying future.

Rome is destroyed.

Huge alien spacecraft fill the sky.

The Colosseum is burning.

Millions of people are running through the streets.

Earth is visible from space, surrounded by darkness and explosions.

### Dialogue

MYSTERIOUS VOICE:

"In seven days... Earth will fall."

Marco opens his eyes.

MARCO:

"What the hell..."

---

## SCENE 03 — NOBODY BELIEVES HIM

Marco tells people what he saw.

Nobody believes him.

### Dialogue

MAN:

"You probably haven't slept enough."

MARCO:

"I'm not joking."

MAN:

"You can see the future now?"

People laugh.

Marco remains alone.

---

## SCENE 04 — ELENA

Marco enters a café.

Elena, 30, a beautiful and intelligent Italian woman,
is working on a laptop.

Marco tells her everything.

### Dialogue

ELENA:

"Tell me exactly what you saw."

MARCO:

"Spaceships. Rome destroyed. Millions of people dead."

Elena looks directly into his eyes.

ELENA:

"I believe you."

Marco is speechless.

---

## SCENE 05 — THE SATELLITE IMAGES

Elena takes Marco to her technology laboratory.

She shows him satellite images.

Strange anomalies are appearing over Europe.

### Dialogue

ELENA:

"Look at this."

MARCO:

"That's the same thing I saw."

ELENA:

"When?"

MARCO:

"Before you showed me."

Elena realizes Marco is telling the truth.

---

## SCENE 06 — SEVEN DAYS

Marco experiences another vision.

A calendar appears.

Seven days.

### Dialogue

MARCO:

"Seven days."

ELENA:

"Until what?"

MARCO:

"Until it begins."

---

## SCENE 07 — THE FIRST SIGNAL

Satellite networks begin failing.

Internet connections collapse.

Airports are shut down.

### Dialogue

NEWS REPORTER:

"Authorities around the world are reporting unexplained
electromagnetic anomalies..."

Marco looks at Elena.

MARCO:

"It's them."

---

## SCENE 08 — THE BLACKOUT

Rome suddenly loses all electricity.

The entire city goes dark.

People look toward the sky.

### Dialogue

ELENA:

"Marco..."

Marco looks upward.

MARCO:

"They're here."

---

## SCENE 09 — THE SPACESHIPS

The clouds open.

A gigantic alien mothership appears.

Then another.

Then hundreds.

The citizens of Rome stare in terror.

### Dialogue

LITTLE GIRL:

"Mom... what are those?"

A massive beam of light crosses the city.

---

## SCENE 10 — THE FIRST ATTACK

Alien spacecraft begin attacking Rome.

Explosions destroy buildings.

People run through the streets.

Marco suddenly freezes.

He sees the future.

### Dialogue

ELENA:

"Where do we go?"

MARCO:

"Left! Now!"

They turn left.

An enormous explosion destroys the street behind them.

Elena looks at Marco in shock.

---

## SCENE 11 — MARCO SEES THE FUTURE

Marco begins predicting alien attacks seconds before they happen.

### Dialogue

ELENA:

"How are you doing this?"

MARCO:

"I don't know."

ELENA:

"Then keep doing it."

---

## SCENE 12 — THE HIDEOUT

Marco and Elena hide inside an abandoned building.

### Dialogue

ELENA:

"Are you sure we can trust what you see?"

MARCO:

"No."

ELENA:

"Then why do you keep going?"

MARCO:

"Because if I stop... everyone dies."

---

## SCENE 13 — ELENA'S DEATH

Marco looks at Elena.

He suddenly sees another future.

Elena is dead.

Marco screams.

### Dialogue

MARCO:

"NO!"

ELENA:

"Marco! What did you see?"

Marco struggles to speak.

MARCO:

"You."

ELENA:

"What?"

MARCO:

"I saw you die."

---

## SCENE 14 — THE COMPUTER

An abandoned computer suddenly turns on.

The screen becomes blue.

### Dialogue

COMPUTER:

"Marco."

MARCO:

"Who are you?"

---

## SCENE 15 — NEXA

A mysterious humanoid digital face appears.

### Dialogue

NEXA:

"My name is NEXA."

MARCO:

"How do you know my name?"

NEXA:

"Because I have already seen you die."

CUT TO BLACK.

### END OF EPISODE 01 — PART ONE
